About Me

Principal Research Fellow at IDLab, imec – University of Antwerp

I am a Principal Research Fellow at IDLab, imec – University of Antwerp, working on sustainable and intelligent connected systems. My research lies at the intersection of low-power wireless communication, energy-neutral and zero-energy devices, embedded intelligence, TinyML, and next-generation IoT systems.

My work focuses on developing energy-efficient technologies that combine communication, computing, sensing, and intelligence for real-world applications. I am particularly interested in energy-neutral computing, low-power communication, intermittent computing, embedded AI, and data-driven situational awareness for future wireless and IoT systems.
