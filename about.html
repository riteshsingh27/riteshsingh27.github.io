About Me

Principal Research Fellow & Principal Investigator at IDLab, imec – University of Antwerp

I work at the intersection of low-power wireless systems, embedded intelligence, and sustainable IoT, with a particular interest in making Zero-Energy and Energy-Neutral Devices more capable, intelligent, and scalable. A central question in my research is how we can build devices that do more with increasingly limited and sometimes unpredictable energy resources.

My research spans energy-aware communication and computing, embedded and edge AI, intermittent systems, TinyML/TinyOL, energy harvesting, and distributed edge intelligence. I am particularly interested in systems that can adapt their sensing, computation, learning, and communication according to the energy available to them, rather than assuming continuous power and connectivity.

This naturally extends from individual devices to larger networks. I explore how highly constrained devices can operate reliably at scale, how intelligence can be distributed across devices, edge, and network infrastructure, and how these systems can eventually become an integral part of future wireless and 6G networks.

My work combines fundamental research, experimental systems, and industry collaboration. I enjoy working across the boundaries of communication, computing, and intelligence turning research ideas into systems that can be evaluated in realistic environments and, ultimately, contribute to more sustainable and intelligent connected technologies.
