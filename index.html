About Me

Principal Research Fellow & Principal Investigator at IDLab, imec – University of Antwerp

I work at the intersection of low-power wireless systems, embedded intelligence, and sustainable IoT, with a focus on making Zero-Energy and Energy-Neutral Devices more capable, intelligent, and scalable. My research spans energy-aware communication and computing, intermittent systems, TinyML/TinyOL, and distributed edge intelligence, with the goal of enabling devices that can adapt their sensing, computing, learning, and communication to their available energy.
