About Me

Postdoctoral Researcher at IDLab, imec University of Antwerp

I work on sustainable wireless systems, TinyML, biotic communication, and next-generation IoT infrastructure. My research brings together communication, sensing, intelligence, and sustainability to develop adaptive and energy-efficient systems for future IoT and wireless networks.
